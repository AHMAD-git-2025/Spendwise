Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about working with SpendWise.

Do I need to connect my bank account? +

No. SpendWise does not ask for your banking login, debit card, or sensitive credentials. You enter transactions on your own terms with complete privacy.

Can I delete my financial records at any point? +

Yes. Go to Settings > Data & Privacy to wipe your financial data immediately or delete your entire account permanently.

Can I export or print my financial reports? +

Yes. Navigate to the Reports tab and click "Print Report" to generate an executive printable summary of your income, expenses, and category allocations.
