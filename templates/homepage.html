Frequently Asked Questions

Is SpendWise free to use? +

Yes. Every account starts with full access to all expense, budget, goal, and report features.

How does the 60-day data retention work? +

SpendWise maintains an automated 60-day retention cycle from your account creation date to preserve user privacy. You can also wipe your data at any time via Settings.

Can I manage multiple income sources? +

Yes. Add salary, investments, freelance payouts, and gifts, tracking them together or filtered by month.

Does SpendWise work smoothly on mobile? +

SpendWise is fully responsive and optimized for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide screens.
